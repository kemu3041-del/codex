Company Profile
Founded in 1988, NANFU has grown from a state-owned battery factory into one of the world’s leading small battery and power solution manufacturers.
  • China’s No.1 battery brand
  • 33 consecutive years of sales leadership in China
  • 86%+ market share in China’s alkaline battery segment
  • Ranked among the global top alkaline battery manufacturers
  • The world’s largest single-site alkaline battery production base

Headquartered in Nanping, Fujian Province, NANFU operates an integrated system combining R&D, intelligent manufacturing, supply chain management, and global OEM services.

NanFu Battery is driven by continuous technological innovation and customer-oriented product development.
